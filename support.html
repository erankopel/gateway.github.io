Common questions

Voice guidance is not speaking

The app uses your device's own text to speech engine. Check Settings, Accessibility, Text to speech output and confirm an engine is installed and a voice is downloaded. Then turn voice guidance on in the app's Audio and Voice panel.

I cannot hear the binaural beats properly

They need stereo headphones. The effect comes from a slightly different tone in each ear, so through a speaker the two mix in the air and it is lost.

Where is my journal stored, and how do I get it out

On your device only. Use Export on the Journal screen to save a copy as a file. Nothing is uploaded, because the app has no network access at all.

Will I lose my journal if I change phone

If Android backup is switched on, the app's data is included in your device backup and restore. Exporting from the Journal screen first is the reliable route.

How do I delete everything

Delete entries individually on the Journal screen, or clear it all at once with Settings, Apps, Gateway, Storage, Clear data. Uninstalling removes all of it.

The screen turns off during an exercise

It should not. The app holds the screen on while it is open, because exercises run for many minutes without a touch. If it still sleeps, check any aggressive battery saver on your device.

Is this medical treatment

No. See the notice on the home page.
